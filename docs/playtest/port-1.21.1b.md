Ironclad Empire on Minecraft 1.21.1 - what to try (~20-30 minutes)
==================================================================

This build is the whole port (1.20.2 -> 1.21.1). It also covers the storage check from the
earlier "MFC-storage-1.20.2" folder, so you only need to play this one.

Basics
 1. New world, Survival Fortress. Place the campfire. 5 pawns should appear.
    Is the camera looking down at the village when you start?
 2. Cut some trees and dig some ground with the pawns.
    Do the items end up in the campfire's chest? Check the resources screen.
 3. Build a small house blueprint. Is the blueprint preview (the ghost of the building) drawn
    correctly while placing? Do the pawns take blocks from the chests and finish it?
 4. Start a build, cancel it half way: do the unused blocks come back?
 5. Start a build, save & quit to title, load the world again: anything lost?

Screens and drawing (these changed the most in the port)
 6. Open every screen you can find: resources/inventory, professions, buildings, blueprints,
    import/export. Note any that are empty, look wrong, crash, or show tooltips twice.
 7. Selections: drag to dig / build. Are the selection boxes and the task outlines drawn?
 8. Pawn clothes/skins: do pawns look right (colours, hats)?

Gameplay
 9. Hire a profession (e.g. lumberjack) via a building. Does it work?
10. Warriors: hire one, select it, order it to attack a mob. Does it fight?
11. Fisherman if you get that far: is the fishing line drawn?
12. Blueprint editor: edit or create a blueprint, save it, exit. Do you land back where you were?

Reply with "all OK" or a list of what went wrong.

Extra for build "b"
13. Fortress controls that share a key with vanilla (check the controls screen): do both actions work?
14. Save & quit, then quit the game completely: does it close normally (no hang)?
