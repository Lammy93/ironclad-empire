Build "bugfix-1": first batch of bug fixes on top of the 1.21.1 port. Install these 3 jars INSTEAD of the earlier ones.

What changed - please try these:
 1. Survival -> "Manage the village" (fortress mode) -> "Explore your village!": your survival items come back.
    Also try it with a full inventory, armour and something in your off-hand.
 2. Select an area that includes the campfire and dig it: the pawns leave the campfire alone.
 3. You can no longer mine the fortress campfire yourself (it holds the whole fortress).
 4. Let a mob/animal attack an idle warrior or archer: they fight back.
 5. Queue several jobs at once (more than your pawns can take): they all get done, one after another.
 6. Quit the world: does saving still take ~30 seconds? Roughly how long?

Then play normally for a bit and note anything odd.
